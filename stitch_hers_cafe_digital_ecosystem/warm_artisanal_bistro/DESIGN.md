## Brand & Style

The brand identity reflects an artisanal, boutique cafe atmosphere rooted in warmth, culinary joy, and modern metropolitan hospitality. Derived from the inviting presence of the pastry chef mascot and warm bistro lettering, the visual language strikes a balance between approachable charm and elevated presentation. 

### Emotional Persona
- **Warm & Convivial:** Inviting, scented with fresh roast espresso and buttery pastries, evoking a comfortable neighborhood refuge.
- **Crafted & Premium:** Moving deliberately away from the aggressive neon and dense grid clutter of utilitarian delivery aggregators. White spaces, deliberate typography, and tactile surfaces communicate high culinary standards.
- **Youthful & Contemporary:** Softened geometry, delightful badges, and crisp micro-interactions ensure the interface feels energetic and easy to navigate for mobile-first diners.

### Visual Style
The design style blends **Tactile Modernism** with **Editorial Warmth**:
- Generous, organic border radii (`16px` to `24px`) create touch-friendly cards and buttons that feel soft in the hand.
- Cream and espresso background layering eliminates sterile blue-gray hues, maintaining an organic parchment tone.
- High-contrast, editorial serif headers provide boutique distinction, while a structural sans-serif handles fast-paced ordering workflows with clarity.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from the cafe experience: rich roasted beans, golden pastries, fresh botanical garnishes, and warm steamed milk.

### Palette Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary (`#3D2317` / Deep Espresso):** Applied to primary actions, key active navigation states, strong headings, and structural elements. It replaces generic black with rich dark roast undertones.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` / Terracotta Glaze):** Used for primary conversion accents, promotional banners, active order callouts, and interactive highlights. An auxiliary warm caramel tone (`#D97706`) serves as a highlight accent for star ratings and baker's badges.
- **Tertiary (`#4A7C59` / Fresh Sage Green):** Reserved for dietary clarity, vegetarian tags, fresh ingredient callouts, and positive transaction feedback.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` to `#F5EFEB` / Vanilla Cream):** The underlying foundation of all screens, creating a soft, warm ambiance that avoids eye strain.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` / Porcelain White):** Floating cards, modal sheets, and food detail panels sit cleanly over the cream canvas to frame culinary photography.
- **Text & Borders:**
  - High-emphasis body and display text: `#1E110A` (Dark Chocolate).
  - Subtle borders and dividers: `#EAE3DA` (Oat Linen).
  - Muted labels and secondary body: `#756357` (Warm Mocha Gray).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates clear tension between artisanal editorial heritage and precise digital performance:

- **Headings & Storytelling (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for category headers, dish hero titles, promotional highlights, and welcome moments. Its high-contrast serifs reference printed bistro menus, establishing immediate boutique character.
- **Functional Interface & Pricing (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and spacious. It provides instant legibility for item descriptions, ingredient listings, nutritional tokens, modifiers, and monetary figures (`PKR`).
- **Numbers & Monetary Figures:** Always set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights 600 or 700) using tabular figures (`tnum`) to keep price tags aligned vertically inside menus and cart breakdown sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive fluid model engineered for tactile mobile ordering and refined desktop exploration.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout. Standard margin is `1rem` (16px) with an interior gutter of `1rem`. Touch targets adhere strictly to a minimum bounding box of `48px` x `48px`.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid. Outer margin increases to `2rem` (32px), accommodating split-screen browsing (category side navigation alongside 2-column menu grids).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`. Outer margin expands to `3rem` (48px) with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Internal component padding, grouping badge icons with text labels, and separating dish titles from price tags.
- **Component Padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Internal breathing room for cards, modal dialogs, and slide-up sheets.
- **Section Breaks (`space-xl`):** Vertical separation between distinct menu categories (e.g., "Artisanal Breads & Savories" to "Pastries & Cakes").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on ambient warmth and physical layering rather than cold synthetic dropshadows.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Canvas:** The screen rests on the warm cream neutral (`#FAF7F2`).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Tiles):** White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) float above the canvas bounded by a soft ghost border (`1px solid #EAE3DA`) and an ambient, warm-tinted shadow:
  - *Standard Card Shadow:* `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(61, 35, 23, 0.06)`
  - *Hover / Lifted Card Shadow:* `0 8px 30px -6px rgba(61, 35, 23, 0.12)`
- **Floating Controls (Sticky Cart Bar & Bottom Navigation):** Elevated above all menu surfaces with a subtle backdrop blur:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
  - Filter: `blur(12px)`
  - Border Top: `1px solid #EAE3DA`
  - Shadow: `0 -4px 24px rgba(61, 35, 23, 0.08)`
- **Modals & Customizer Sheets:** Elevated on the highest tier with an espresso-tinted backdrop scrim:
  - Scrim: `rgba(30, 17, 10, 0.45)`
  - Sheet Shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(61, 35, 23, 0.24)`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates rounded, organic forms that mirror the curves of bakery ceramics, pastry domes, and warm cafe architecture.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested quantity selectors, and auxiliary controls.
- **Primary Interactive & Card Radius (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Standard for product menu cards, hero promotion panels, and modal containers.
- **Hero & Modal Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Used for bottom drawer sheets, checkout summary modules, and category hero banners.
- **Capsule Radii (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for dietary tags, badges, category filter chips, and primary floating call-to-actions (e.g., "Add to Tray").

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Add to Tray / Checkout):** Deep espresso (`#3D2317`) background with crisp white typography. Height is fixed at `48px` minimum on mobile, styled with pill (`rounded-full`) or `16px` curvature. Tap feedback features an inward scale transition (`scale-98`).
- **Secondary Action (Customize / Details):** Cream fill (`#F5EFEB`) with dark chocolate text (`#1E110A`) and an oat border (`#EAE3DA`).
- **Tertiary Accent (Promotions / Quick Reorder):** Terracotta (`#C86D51`) background with pure white typography, drawing the eye toward specials.

### Menu Cards
- Built on a pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card structure with `16px` corners and oat borders.
- Aspect ratio of food photography is strictly `4:3` or `1:1` with rounded top corners.
- Cards feature an integrated bottom row containing the price in bold tabular digits (`PKR 850`) alongside an ergonomic plus-icon button (`44px` target) for instant cart additions.

### Badges & Dietary Tags
- Built as pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) chips with subtle tinted fills and dark borders:
  - **Vegetarian / Vegan:** Sage green background (`#EBF3EE`), sage green border (`#C2DEC9`), sage green text (`#2A5235`). Accompanied by a leaf icon.
  - **Chef's Special:** Terracotta background (`#FAF0EC`), warm rust text (`#A34B32`).
  - **Spicy:** Warm chili tint (`#FDEFEF`), deep ruby text (`#991B1B`).
  - **Best Value:** Caramel gold background (`#FEF7EB`), dark amber text (`#92400E`).

### Category Filter Chips
- Horizontal scrolling pill tabs (`height: 40px`, `rounded-full`).
- Inactive state: White fill, `#EAE3DA` border, `#756357` text.
- Active state: `#3D2317` fill, no border, pure white text, accompanied by an animated warm dot indicator.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Text inputs use a soft vanilla fill (`#FDFCFA`) with an inset oat border (`1.5px solid #EAE3DA`). On focus, the border transitions to `#3D2317` without abrupt rings.
- Quantity steppers feature rounded capsule enclosures with distinct plus/minus hit zones (`36px` x `36px`) and high-contrast espresso counters.

### Mobile Sticky Tray Bar
- A persistent bottom pill bar that reveals subtotal and current item count, styled with espresso background, white text, and a caramel action button.
